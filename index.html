How to prepare

  1. Find your levelDo Full Simulation 1 without help. Your result shows which word types cost you points.
  2. Repair the basicsWork on your weakest type on the Grammar and Practice pages.
  3. Learn words daily15 minutes of C-test gaps in the vocabulary trainer, then “my mistakes”.
  4. Fix your mistakesAfter every test, practise the wrong gaps on My mistakes.
  5. Simulate the examOne full simulation every second day. Easy gaps first, never leave a gap empty.
